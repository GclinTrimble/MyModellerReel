# MyModellerReel 0.27.1: a view bar that fits

## The view bar never wraps

With room, the bar over the view shows every button. As the view or a pane gets narrower, display, the pane's own buttons, views and projection merge into their drop-downs, then the last controls fold one by one into a **More** menu.

![One view, then four](https://github.com/GclinTrimble/MyModellerReel/releases/download/v0.27.1/view-bar.gif)

- The **Views** menu always holds everything: standard views, road drawings, projection, wireframe, maximise, own window, camera mark, scenes and layout.
- With several views, each pane's bar shows just its number (plus the stepping buttons on road drawings), and keeps clear of the panel tabs on a right-edge pane.
